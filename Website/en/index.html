Ready in three screens

What CoreTend promises, Full Disk Access explained in one sentence (CoreTend notices when you allow it), and you start.

A greenhouse that lives

Motion with a cause

Roots descend as files are read. A moved file falls as a leaf into the Trash. A file that stays says why.

Alive, never busy

Leaves sway and pollen drifts while the window is in front — drawn by macOS itself, for almost no energy — and everything rests the moment you look away.

Calm on request

Reduce Motion, Low Power Mode or one switch in Settings: the greenhouse stands still, every piece of information still in place.

What CoreTend will never do

Erase for good

Every move goes to the macOS Trash, after your review. Undo puts it back.

Scare you

No health score, no alarm, no invented figure. Only what macOS measures, explained in plain words.

Watch you

No account, no telemetry. The only network request asks the site whether an update exists — and you can turn it off.
