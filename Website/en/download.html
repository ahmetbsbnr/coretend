New in 2.0

A complete rebuild: the living greenhouse design, eight tools, a three-page welcome, Applications with sizes and sorting, Explore that walks into folders, Integrity for a whole folder at once, recoverable space in Duplicates, memory in use, and a Settings window in four tabs.

Coming from 1.x? 2.0 replaces it. Your 1.x data are not touched; you can import its preferences and exclusions from Settings.
