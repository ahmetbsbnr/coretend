# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mac users who want to understand storage use, review clutter, inspect installed
applications, and monitor system health without surrendering control to an
opaque cleaner. They need a calm path from download through first action, with
clear disclosure that the public beta is unsigned, not notarized, Apple
Silicon-only, and intended for macOS 14 or later.

## Product Purpose

CoreTend is an open-source macOS utility that keeps analysis local, explains
findings before acting, defaults to dry run, and uses recoverable removal where
supported. The public site must make the real application understandable,
provide a safe graphical installation path, and distinguish released 0.9.0
capabilities from later work.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and caring. The voice is factual and reassuring without hiding
limitations, creating fear, or making absolute promises.

## Anti-references

- Do not clone TalkInk or reproduce its copy, palette, typography, media, code,
  or animation choreography. Adapt only general principles supported by an
  independent CoreTend composition.
- Avoid generic SaaS card walls, glass effects, decorative gradients, heavy
  parallax, scroll hijacking, and motion that delays access to content.
- Avoid invented product UI, simulated macOS security dialogs, fabricated
  metrics, and unsupported claims.
- Avoid unnecessary affiliation disclaimers or language that implies approval
  by Apple or another company.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real product and label the version represented.
2. Make safety legible at the decision point, especially download, Gatekeeper,
   permissions, and file removal.
3. Keep essential content available on first paint, without JavaScript, and
   with reduced motion.
4. Use CoreTend's Living System identity consistently while letting each
   section serve a distinct narrative purpose.
5. Prefer measured evidence over superlatives, and local control over vague
   reassurance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target reasonable WCAG 2.2 AA conformance. Support keyboard navigation, visible
focus, semantic landmarks, useful alternative text, captions or transcripts
for meaningful video, 200% zoom, touch targets, dark mode, high-contrast text,
`prefers-reduced-motion`, and content access when JavaScript is disabled.
