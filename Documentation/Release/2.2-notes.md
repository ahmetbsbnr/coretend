# CoreTend 2.2

**See what fills your Mac. Clear it safely. Now on every Mac.**

- **Intel Macs too**: one universal app, macOS 14 or later.
- **Shortcuts and Siri**: Get Free Space, Get Space to Clear, Open CoreTend. They read and open;
  none moves a file.
- **Widget**: free space and what the last Clean found, on the desktop.
- **Finder**: right-click a folder › Analyze with CoreTend. Or drop it on the Dock icon.
- **System features, optional**: tidy /Library/Caches and turn third-party system services on or
  off, through a small signed helper you install from Settings › System and can remove anytime.

Everything still goes to the Trash, with Undo; nothing is erased; no telemetry.

**Install:** open the DMG and move CoreTend to Applications, or `brew install --cask ahmetbsbnr/coretend/coretend`.
From 2.1: CoreTend updates itself.

macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
